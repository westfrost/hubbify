# Privacy Policy for HUBBIFY

*Last updated: September 25, 2026 (Windows version added)*

HUBBIFY is an app for Android and Windows that plays a "dong" sound at random intervals.

## What data is collected?

**None.** HUBBIFY does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
- The app does not use the internet.
- Your settings (e.g. number of people and minutes) are stored only on your device: on Android in
  the app's own storage (deleted when you uninstall the app), on Windows in the file
  `%APPDATA%\DongRadio\indstillinger.txt`.

## Permissions (Android)

- **Notifications:** show that the app is running and provide a Stop button.
- **Foreground service and keeping the device awake:** so the DONGs can sound while the app is in
  the background or the screen is off.
- **Microphone (optional, off by default):** only if you turn on "Neon på bassen", the app listens
  for bass beats in the room so the logo can glow in time with the music. The sound is analysed on
  the phone in real time while the app is open. It is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

None of these permissions are used to collect data.

## Windows

- To make the logo glow in time with the music, the Windows version analyses the sound that the PC
  itself is playing (its speaker output) in real time. The sound is never recorded, stored or sent
  anywhere, and the microphone is not used.
- To lower the volume of Spotify while a sound plays, the app changes Spotify's volume in the
  Windows volume mixer and restores it afterwards.

## Children

HUBBIFY is a drinking game intended for adults aged 18 and over only.

## Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent to the contact email shown on the app's Google Play
page.
